Privacy Policy.

A short note on how this JAM website handles information.

Website

This site is informational.

The JAM website is a static public website. It does not provide user accounts, collect payment information, or ask visitors to enter personal information directly on this site.

External forms

When JAM links to Google Forms, email, or other third-party services, information submitted there is handled by those services and by JAM officers for the stated event or membership purpose.

Event records

Public event pages may include photos, event descriptions, sponsor acknowledgments, and archive information to document JAM activities.

Contact

Questions or requests about website content can be sent to jam-hq@mit.edu.
